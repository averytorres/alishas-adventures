# Alisha and the Lost Star

**Status:** English story draft for review. No voices have been generated.

**Library preview:** A little dragon has lost the star that lights the way home. Explore moonlit places with Princess Alisha, follow gentle clues, and discover different ways to help.

**Starting screen:** castle_gate

## castle_gate

Princess Alisha stepped outside just as the sky turned purple. A little dragon sat beside the castle gate. His wings drooped. 'My guiding star fell from the sky,' he said. 'Without it, I cannot find my way home.' Alisha looked up. One small space in the sky was dark. A silver sparkle floated past the gate, then vanished around the bend. The dragon gave Alisha a hopeful smile. 'Will you look with me?' The path divided ahead. One way crossed a wooden bridge. The other led into the moon garden. Both looked full of surprises.
