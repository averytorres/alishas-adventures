# Spoken choice visibility — November 5, 2026

During narration, each choice row now scrolls into the comfortable reading area when necessary and highlights while its recording plays. A repeated choice is identified by its line ID, so its speaker button or hold action highlights that choice rather than the situation. The same code handles screens with two or three choices.

The updated Android draft was signed and installed on the Android test phone. On the phone, the second choice was visible and highlighted during the full screen reading. Tapping the first choice's speaker button highlighted the first choice. Eight focused tests and the narration cancellation check passed. The recorded route was checked in English; Spanish and Gujarati use the same choice display logic but were not separately watched for this change.
